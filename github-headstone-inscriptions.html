What Are Headstone Inscriptions?

Headstone inscriptions, also known as epitaphs, are the words engraved on a grave marker or monument. These inscriptions can be as simple as a name and dates, or as elaborate as a poem, scripture, or personal message. The inscription is often the first thing visitors see, making it a powerful way to honor and remember the deceased.

Popular Types of Headstone Inscriptions

There are many ways to personalize a headstone inscription. Here are some of the most common types:

  • Basic Information: Full name, birth date, and death date.
  • Relationship Titles: Beloved Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, etc.
  • Personal Quotes: Favorite sayings or mottos.
  • Religious Verses: Bible verses, prayers, or spiritual messages.
  • Poetic Lines: Excerpts from poems or literature.

Tips for Choosing the Right Inscription

  • Consider the personality and values of your loved one.
  • Think about what message you want to leave for future generations.
  • Check with the cemetery for any restrictions on length or content.
  • Consult with family members to gather ideas and reach consensus.
