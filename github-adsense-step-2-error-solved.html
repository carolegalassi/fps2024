What is the Adsense Step 2 Error?

The Adsense Step 2 error typically appears when you are trying to complete the second step of your Adsense application. This step often involves connecting your site, verifying your identity, or placing the Adsense code. The error can be vague, sometimes simply stating that something went wrong or that your site cannot be reviewed. This can be caused by several factors, including incomplete site setup, policy violations, or technical issues with your website.

Common Causes and Solutions

Below is a quick reference table outlining the most frequent causes of the Adsense Step 2 error and the recommended solutions. Use this table to identify your issue and take the appropriate action.

Cause Symptoms Solution
Incomplete Site Content Site not fully built or lacks original content Add quality, unique content to all pages
Policy Violations Adsense dashboard shows policy warnings Review and fix policy issues, then reapply
Incorrect Adsense Code Placement Adsense code not detected or placed incorrectly Place code in the <head> section as instructed
