What Is A Funeral Resolution?

A funeral resolution is a formal statement or document that is read aloud during a funeral or memorial service, typically in a religious setting. It honors the life, faith, and legacy of the deceased, while also offering words of comfort and encouragement to the family and congregation. Funeral resolutions are most common in Christian and especially African American church traditions, but the practice is growing in other communities as well.

Unlike a simple obituary or eulogy, a funeral resolution follows a specific structure and is often adopted by a church, organization, or community group. It becomes part of the official church records and serves as a lasting tribute. If you have been asked to write or present a funeral resolution, understanding its purpose and format will help you create a meaningful and respectful document.

Funeral Resolution vs. Obituary vs. Eulogy

It is easy to confuse a funeral resolution with an obituary or a eulogy, but each serves a distinct purpose. The table below highlights the key differences:

Type Purpose Typical Content
Funeral Resolution Formal church record, tribute, and encouragement Faith statements, biographical summary, condolences, official adoption
Obituary Public notice of death and life summary Birth/death dates, survivors, life highlights
Eulogy Personal tribute and memories Stories, personal reflections, impact on others

How to Write a Funeral Resolution

Writing a funeral resolution may seem daunting, but following a clear structure can make the process easier. Here are the typical sections included:

  • Opening Statement: A formal introduction, often beginning with "Whereas" to state the reason for the resolution.
  • Biographical Details: A brief overview of the deceased's life, faith, and contributions.
  • Expressions of Sympathy: Words of comfort and encouragement to the family and congregation.
  • Official Adoption: A closing statement that the resolution is adopted by the church or organization, often including a signature line for the pastor or church clerk.

Why Funeral Resolutions Matter

Funeral resolutions serve several important purposes. They provide spiritual comfort, preserve the memory of the deceased, and offer a formal record for the church or organization. Many families find peace in knowing that their loved one is honored in this lasting way. If you are part of a church or community group, adopting a funeral resolution can also strengthen bonds and show collective support for the family.
