What Is An Urn Vault?

An urn vault is a protective container designed to encase a cremation urn when it is buried in the ground. Many cemeteries require the use of an urn vault to prevent the earth from collapsing around the urn, to protect the urn from moisture and soil pressure, and to maintain the integrity of the burial site over time. Understanding what an urn vault is and why it is important can help families make informed decisions during the cremation and burial planning process.

What Is the Purpose of an Urn Vault?

When a loved one is cremated and their ashes are placed in an urn, families often wish to bury the urn in a cemetery plot. However, the ground can shift, settle, and exert pressure on the urn over time. An urn vault acts as a sturdy barrier, shielding the urn from the weight of the earth and heavy maintenance equipment that may pass over the grave. This not only preserves the urn itself but also prevents the ground above from sinking or caving in, ensuring the burial site remains dignified and level.

Types of Urn Vaults

Urn vaults come in a variety of materials, sizes, and designs to suit different needs and preferences. The most common materials used in urn vault construction are reinforced concrete, high-strength plastic, and metal. Some vaults are simple and utilitarian, while others are decorative and can be personalized with engravings or emblems.

Cemetery Requirements and Regulations

Most cemeteries have specific rules regarding the burial of cremation urns. Many require an urn vault to be used, especially in areas where the ground is unstable or where heavy equipment is used for maintenance. It is important to check with the cemetery before making arrangements, as they may have size, material, or design restrictions for urn vaults. Some cemeteries may even provide vaults as part of their burial services, while others require families to purchase them separately.

How to Choose the Right Urn Vault

When selecting an urn vault, consider the following factors:

  • Material durability and resistance to weather
  • Size compatibility with your chosen urn
  • Cemetery regulations and requirements
  • Personalization options, such as engravings or emblems
  • Budget and aesthetic preferences

Benefits of Using an Urn Vault

Using an urn vault provides peace of mind for families who wish to bury a loved one’s ashes. The vault ensures that the urn remains protected from the elements and from accidental damage caused by ground settling or cemetery equipment. It also helps maintain the appearance of the burial site, preventing unsightly sinking or unevenness. Some urn vaults can be personalized to reflect the personality or legacy of the deceased, offering a meaningful tribute that endures for generations.
