What Is a Homegoing Service?

A homegoing service is a funeral tradition rooted in African American culture, designed to honor the life and spiritual journey of the deceased. Unlike somber funerals, homegoings are often filled with music, joyful remembrances, and expressions of faith. The service acknowledges the pain of loss but focuses on the belief that the loved one is returning home to be with God.

When I went to a homegoing service, I was struck by the vibrant atmosphere. Gospel choirs sang uplifting songs, speakers shared heartfelt stories, and the congregation celebrated the legacy of the departed. The service was both emotional and uplifting, creating a sense of unity and hope.

How To Prepare for a Homegoing Service

Preparation is key to feeling comfortable and respectful at a homegoing service. Here are some tips I found helpful:

  • Dress respectfully, often in dark or subdued colors, but some families may request white or brighter attire to symbolize celebration.
  • Arrive on time, as services may begin with a processional or special music.
  • Be prepared for emotional moments, but also for laughter and joyful memories.
  • Participate in singing or responsive readings if invited.
  • Respect photography or recording rules, as some families prefer privacy.
