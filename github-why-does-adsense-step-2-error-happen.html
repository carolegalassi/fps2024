Understanding the AdSense Step 2 Error

The Step 2 error in AdSense typically appears when Google is unable to verify your site or account during the application process. This error can be confusing, especially since Google does not always provide a detailed explanation. The error often reads something like: We are unable to review your site. Please ensure your site is accessible and meets our policies.

Here are the most common reasons why this error occurs:

  • Your website is not accessible to Google bots (due to server issues, maintenance mode, or geo-blocking).
  • The AdSense code is not properly placed on your site or is missing entirely.
  • Your site is too new, lacks original content, or does not meet AdSense content policies.
  • There are technical issues such as incorrect DNS settings or robots.txt blocking Google.

How to Troubleshoot the Step 2 Error

If you encounter the Step 2 error, follow these steps:

  1. Verify your website is online and accessible from different locations.
  2. Double-check that the AdSense code snippet is correctly placed on your site.
  3. Review your robots.txt file and make sure it does not block Googlebot.
  4. Ensure your site has enough original content and complies with all AdSense policies.
  5. Wait at least 48 hours after making changes before reapplying.
