What Is a Homegoing Service Program?

A homegoing service program is a printed or digital booklet that outlines the order of events, tributes, and important information for a homegoing ceremony. Rooted in African American tradition, the homegoing service celebrates the life, faith, and legacy of the departed. Unlike a somber funeral, a homegoing is often filled with music, personal stories, and uplifting messages, reflecting the belief that the loved one is going home to eternal rest.

Creating a thoughtful homegoing service program helps guide attendees through the ceremony and provides a cherished keepsake for family and friends. It typically includes the order of service, obituary, photographs, acknowledgments, and sometimes poems or scriptures.

How to Personalize Your Program

Personal touches make a homegoing service program truly special. Consider including favorite scriptures, meaningful poems, or song lyrics that reflect your loved one’s spirit. You can also add a timeline of major life events, a collage of photos, or a section for attendees to write their own memories. Digital and Print Options

Today, homegoing service programs can be distributed both in print and digitally. Printed booklets are traditional and provide a tangible keepsake, while digital versions can be shared with distant friends and family. Many families choose to do both.
