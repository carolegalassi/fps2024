What Is a Burial Vault?

A burial vault is a protective outer container that surrounds the casket in the ground. Its primary purpose is to prevent the grave from collapsing and to shield the casket from soil and water. Many cemeteries require burial vaults to maintain the appearance and safety of the grounds. Vaults are typically made from concrete, metal, or composite materials, and their construction quality can vary widely.

Factors Affecting Burial Vaults Cost

  • Material: Concrete is the most common and affordable, while metal or lined vaults are more expensive.
  • Design and Finish: Decorative finishes, personalization, and upgraded linings increase the price.
  • Cemetery Requirements: Some cemeteries mandate specific types of vaults, which can affect your choices and costs.
  • Location: Prices can vary significantly by region and cemetery.
  • Funeral Home or Direct Purchase: Buying through a funeral home may include additional service fees, while direct purchase from a manufacturer can sometimes save money.

How to Compare and Save

To ensure you are getting the best value, always request a detailed price list from your funeral provider. Compare the costs of different vault types, and ask if you can supply your own vault. Some families choose to purchase vaults online or from third-party suppliers. Be sure to confirm with the cemetery that your chosen vault meets their requirements before making a purchase.
