Understanding Funeral Caskets

Funeral caskets serve as both a final resting place and a means of expressing respect for the deceased. They come in a variety of styles, materials, and price ranges. Whether you are planning ahead or making arrangements for a loved one, it is important to consider factors such as durability, appearance, and budget.

There are two main categories of caskets: burial caskets and cremation caskets. Burial caskets are typically constructed from wood or metal and are designed for interment. Cremation caskets are often made from materials that combust cleanly and may be simpler in design. Many families also choose caskets based on religious or cultural preferences.

Key Considerations When Choosing a Casket

  • Budget: Funeral caskets can range from a few hundred to several thousand dollars. Set a realistic budget before shopping.
  • Material: Decide between wood, metal, or eco-friendly options based on your preferences and values.
  • Features: Consider interior fabric, handles, sealing options, and custom engravings.
  • Funeral Home Policies: Federal law allows you to purchase a casket from any source, not just the funeral home.
