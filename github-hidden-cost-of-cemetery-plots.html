What Is a Cemetery Plot?

A cemetery plot is a designated piece of land within a cemetery where an individual or family can be buried. While the concept seems simple, the process of purchasing a plot can be complex and is often accompanied by a range of additional costs that are not always disclosed upfront.

Common Hidden Costs of Cemetery Plots

Many families are surprised to learn that the price of a cemetery plot is just the beginning. Here are some of the most common hidden costs associated with cemetery plots:

Cost TypeTypical RangeDescription
Opening and Closing Fee$500 - $2,500Covers the labor and equipment to dig and refill the grave
Grave Liner or Vault$800 - $3,000Required by many cemeteries to prevent ground settling
Perpetual Care Fee$200 - $1,000Funds ongoing maintenance of the cemetery grounds

Other Potential Fees

  • Headstone installation and foundation fees
  • Marker or monument permit fees
  • Transfer or deed recording fees
  • Weekend or holiday burial surcharges
  • Additional charges for double-depth or companion plots

How to Avoid Surprises When Buying a Cemetery Plot

To avoid being caught off guard by hidden costs, it is important to ask the right questions and request a full price list from the cemetery before making any commitments. Here are some tips:

  • Request a detailed breakdown of all potential fees in writing
  • Ask if grave liners or vaults are mandatory and what options are available
  • Inquire about ongoing maintenance or perpetual care charges
  • Clarify headstone or marker installation rules and costs
  • Check if there are extra fees for burials on weekends or holidays
