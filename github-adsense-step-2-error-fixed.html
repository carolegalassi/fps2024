Understanding the Adsense Step 2 Error

The Adsense Step 2 error typically appears after you submit your website for Adsense approval. This error can be caused by a variety of issues, including incomplete site setup, missing privacy policies, or technical problems with your website code. It is important to carefully review your site and ensure all Google requirements are met before resubmitting your application.

Common causes of the Adsense Step 2 error include:

  • Website not fully launched or accessible to the public
  • Missing or incomplete About, Contact, or Privacy Policy pages
  • Insufficient original content or thin pages
  • Technical issues such as broken links or slow loading times
  • Incorrect Adsense code placement

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Table

Step Action Result
1 Verify all required pages (About, Contact, Privacy Policy) are present and accessible Ensures compliance with Adsense policies
2 Check for original, high-quality content on every page Improves chances of approval and user experience
3 Place the Adsense code exactly as instructed in the site header Allows Adsense to verify your site correctly
