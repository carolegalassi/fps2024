What Is a Funeral Program?

A funeral program is a printed or digital booklet distributed at a memorial or funeral service. It typically includes the order of service, photos, biographical information, poems, and messages from the family. The program serves as both a guide for attendees and a cherished keepsake for years to come. Good funeral program design ensures the document is easy to follow, visually appealing, and respectful of the individual being honored.

Design Tips for a Memorable Program

  • Choose fonts and colors that reflect the personality of your loved one, but keep readability in mind.
  • Use high-quality photos and scan images at a resolution of at least 300 dpi for print.
  • Balance text and images so the program does not feel cluttered.
  • Consider using templates to streamline the process and ensure a professional look.
  • Proofread carefully and ask another family member to review before printing.
